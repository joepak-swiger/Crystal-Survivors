# Asset provenance and use

`assets/cloud-walk-placeholder.png` is the 256 × 768 Cloud walk atlas supplied by the project owner in this task. It is sliced into an 8 row × 4 column animation grid of 64 × 96 pixel frames and rendered with nearest-neighbor scaling. The owner also supplied a larger Cloud sprite sheet and a Final Fantasy VII character lineup as visual references; the larger sheet has a white background and was not used as a runtime texture.

The small atlas has aligned cells and transparent backgrounds, but its rows repeat a similar side-facing pose. Eight row directions are supported by the animation configuration, but the art does not contain eight distinct directional animations. Replace it with cleared artwork before any commercial release. Cloud and Final Fantasy VII are Square Enix intellectual property; this placeholder is fan art and no distribution rights are claimed here. Goblin, Bomb, arena, effects, and UI are drawn in code for this prototype.
